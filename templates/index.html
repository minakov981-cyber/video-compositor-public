How it works

Three steps. No editing skills.

Everything happens in the browser. Nothing to install.

1

Drop in clips and music

Add your clips and an audio track. Drag the clips into the order you want and choose how long each one plays, or keep them at their original length.

2

Set the look

Add a caption, pick the format, clip length and transitions. Trim or speed up any clip if you want. Or keep the defaults.

3

Create, then reshuffle

Hit Create Video and download the result. Want another version? New Variation reshuffles the middle clips, with no re-uploading.

Captions

Your text on top of the video. Choose the font, size, colour, position and a box or shadow behind it.

Transitions

Hard cuts, or fades, slides, wipes and zooms between clips. Pick one, or let each cut be random.

Handy tools

FAQ

Questions, answered.

What counts as one free video?

Each time you press Create Video counts as one. Every video then comes with up to {{ free_variations }} New Variations at no extra cost, so {{ free_generations }} free videos means up to {{ free_generations * (free_variations + 1) }} finished versions.

Is there a watermark on free videos?

No. Free and Pro videos look exactly the same.

How long can a video be?

Up to {{ max_video_seconds // 60 }} minutes. The editor shows the estimated length before you render, so you'll know right away.

What files can I upload?

Any common video format, including MP4 and MOV straight from an iPhone or Android phone, and audio such as MP3, M4A or WAV. Clips can have different sizes and frame rates. ClipSnap evens them out.

Do you keep my videos?

Only briefly. Uploads and finished videos are deleted automatically about {{ file_retention_hours }} hours after they're made, so download what you want to keep. Your clips are never used for anything else. See the Privacy Policy.

How do I cancel Pro?

Anytime from your Account page. Pro stays active until the end of the month you've paid for and you won't be charged again.

Can I get a refund?

Yes. If ClipSnap isn't for you, ask within 14 days of your first payment and you'll get a full refund. Details are in the Refund Policy.

Do I need to install anything?

No. ClipSnap runs in your browser on a computer or phone. Videos are rendered on our servers, so even an older laptop works fine.
