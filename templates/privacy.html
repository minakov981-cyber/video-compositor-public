3. Cookies and local storage

We use one essential cookie to keep you signed in (for up to 30 days). The app also remembers small interface preferences, such as the last tab you opened, in your browser's local storage. These are needed for the service to work, so no consent banner is required. Cloudflare Turnstile, our anti-bot check on the signup form, may process technical data from your browser to tell people from bots.

4. Who we share data with

We use these providers to run Clip Snap. They process data only as needed for their service:

  • Railway: hosting of the app, database and files (servers may be in the USA).
  • Stripe and Link: payments and subscriptions. For purchases, Link is the reseller and processes your payment data under its own privacy policy.
  • Resend: sending account emails (login and confirmation links).
  • Cloudflare: domain, DNS and Turnstile bot protection.
  • Sentry: error monitoring. When something breaks, it receives a technical error report with your account number (not your email or IP address).
  • Google Fonts: fonts used on the pages; your browser loads them from Google, which receives your IP address.

Some of these providers are outside the European Economic Area. Transfers are covered by the EU Standard Contractual Clauses or the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.
